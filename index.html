Why PARAMPARA

Some stories are
disappearing.

Across generations, countless traditions, memories and oral histories remain undocumented, fragmented or vulnerable to being forgotten.

01

Cultural memories are disappearing

As generations change, unique stories, traditions and memories can disappear before they are ever documented.

02

Oral histories are difficult to preserve

Valuable knowledge often exists only through spoken memories, making it difficult to preserve and pass forward.

03

Digital content lacks provenance

Existing digital archives often lack a clear source, evidence trail or verification history behind the information they contain.

How PARAMPARA Works

From memory to legacy.

A simple journey that transforms personal memories into verified digital heritage.

Record

Capture stories, memories and traditions through voice, text, images or video.

Verify

Review sources, evidence and community knowledge to establish authenticity.

Preserve

Securely preserve cultural memories in a structured digital archive.

Discover

Let future generations explore and reconnect with the heritage of their communities.
