PARAMPARA / 01 LIVING HERITAGE

Preserving what makes us who we are.

Some stories are written in books. Others live in the people who remember them.

Parampara is a digital space created to discover, document and preserve the cultural heritage that connects generations.

From traditions and folk stories to art, crafts, languages, festivals and places of historical significance, Parampara brings these fragments of culture together in one accessible space.
