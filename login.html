PARAMPARA ACCESS

One archive. Different responsibilities.

Every role has carefully defined permissions to protect the integrity and provenance of cultural knowledge.

👤

Community Member

Contributor
  • Record oral history
  • Upload audio
  • Give/manage consent
  • Correct transcripts
  • Approve translations
  • Set access restrictions
  • Request removal
🔬

Researcher

Research Access
  • Search public archives
  • Request restricted material
  • Analyze transcripts
  • Explore themes
  • Download permitted datasets
  • Cannot change original testimony
🔤

Linguist

Language Specialist
  • Compare linguistic variants
  • Examine transcripts
  • Review translations
  • Add linguistic annotations
🏺

Archaeologist

Heritage Research
  • Search relevant oral histories
  • Explore historical place narratives
  • Link oral accounts with research
  • Request restricted recordings
🗄️

Archivist

Archive Management
  • Manage metadata
  • Maintain versions
  • Review provenance
  • Manage archive records
  • Monitor preservation status
🏛️

Institution

Organizational Access
  • Manage institutional collections
  • Approve researchers
  • Manage organizational access
  • Generate reports
🎓

Student

Educational Access
  • Search public content
  • Listen to permitted recordings
  • Read transcripts
  • Explore educational collections
