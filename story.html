Story Transcript

The voice behind the memory.

Recorded oral account

ଆମ ଗାଁର ବୁଢ଼ା ଲୋକମାନେ କହୁଥିଲେ, ସମୁଦ୍ରକୁ ଯିବା ପୂର୍ବରୁ ଏହି ଗୀତ ଗାଇବା ଆମର ପୁରୁଣା ପରମ୍ପରା ଥିଲା।

ଗୀତଟି କେବଳ ଗୀତ ନଥିଲା। ଏହା ଆମକୁ ସାହସ ଦେଉଥିଲା ଏବଂ ସମୁଦ୍ରର ରାସ୍ତା ମନେ ପକାଇ ଦେଉଥିଲା।

The elders of our village used to say that singing this song before going to sea was an old tradition.

It was more than a song. It gave the fishermen courage and reminded them of the path across the sea.
