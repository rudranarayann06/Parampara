WHY IT MATTERS

When a voice disappears, a piece of history can disappear with it.

Cultural heritage is often carried through people rather than institutions. Once a story, practice or language disappears from everyday life, recovering it can become almost impossible.

01

Cultural memories are disappearing

Generational knowledge can fade when it is no longer passed forward.

02

Oral histories are difficult to preserve

Stories carried through voices rarely have a permanent, accessible record.

03

Context can be lost

A recording without its language, community, location and provenance loses part of its meaning.

PARAMPARA preserves not just the story, but the context behind it — who shared it, where it came from, what language it belongs to, and how it was verified.

CULTURAL RESPECT

Preserve with permission.

Cultural heritage belongs to people and communities. PARAMPARA puts consent and access choices at the center of preservation.

Consent

The storyteller decides whether their story can be preserved.

Community

Contributors can identify the community associated with a story.

Access

Choose whether a story is public, restricted or private.

Control

Contributors can request corrections or removal.

Preservation should never come at the cost of ownership, dignity or consent.
